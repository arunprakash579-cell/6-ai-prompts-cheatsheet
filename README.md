# 6 AI Prompts for Better Answers (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini)

A free cheat sheet from **Learn With Me Arun**: 6 copy-paste prompts that get you clearer, more personal answers from any AI chat, plus one that makes the AI find the blind spots in your plan instead of just saying "super idea".

> In short: copy these 6 prompts into any AI chat and you will get far fewer generic answers.

## The 6 prompts
1. **Explain like I'm 10** – complex topics in simple words.
2. **Ask me 5 clarifying questions first** – answers that fit your situation.
3. **Show an example** – match a structure and tone you like.
4. **Save your context once (Memory)** – stop repeating your background (Claude Skills notes included).
5. **Don't stop until…** – define "done" so long tasks get finished.
6. **Find my blind spots** – make the AI challenge you, not just agree.

## Privacy first
Use dummy data. Strip names, employee IDs, PAN, Aadhaar, account numbers and UPI IDs. Never upload a full bank statement or confidential company data.
